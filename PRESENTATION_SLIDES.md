## Slide 2: Problem Context & Industry Impact
* **The Challenge**: Modern cyber threats (zero-day backdoors, supply chain attacks) evolve rapidly; SOC analysts suffer from severe alert fatigue.
* **The Dilemma**: Transmitting raw enterprise network logs to cloud LLMs violates GDPR, HIPAA, and corporate data privacy rules.
* **Our Mission**: Build an intelligent, parallel multi-agent SOC platform that guarantees **0% PII leakage** while executing automated incident response in seconds.

---

## Slide 3: Architecture & Technical Innovation
* **Local Privacy Guardrail**: Pre-LLM PII scrubber masks IPs, emails, credentials, and financial data before API calls.
* **Local MCP SQLite DB**: Serves local threat feeds, user baselines, and playbooks via Model Context Protocol tools.
* **Parallel Multi-Agent Framework**:
  - `Phi-4-reasoning`: Master Orchestration & Containment Strategy
  - `GPT-4o`: Multimodal Vision OCR Log Inspection & Anomaly Detection
  - `DeepSeek-V3`: Fast Threat Intel Synthesis & Forensic Documentation
* **Multimodal Copilot**: GPT-4o Vision OCR for terminal screenshots + HTML5 Speech-to-Text & Speaker TTS readbacks.
